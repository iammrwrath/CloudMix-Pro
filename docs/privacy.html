Privacy Policy for CloudMix Pro

Effective Date: November 27, 2026

CloudMix Pro ("we", "our", or "the Application") is a desktop DJ application developed by iammrwrath. This Privacy Policy explains how CloudMix Pro accesses, uses, stores, and protects your information when you connect your Google / YouTube Music account.

1. Information We Access

When you sign in with Google OAuth, CloudMix Pro requests the read-only scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.readonly. This allows the application to:

  • Read your YouTube Music playlists and liked/saved tracks so you can browse and load them into DJ decks inside CloudMix Pro.
  • Display your connected Google account email/profile status in the Settings panel.

2. How We Use and Store Your Data

All OAuth tokens and playlist metadata are stored strictly locally on your device inside your application userData directory (ytm-auth.json) and local browser storage. CloudMix Pro does not operate a remote backend server that collects, logs, or stores your Google credentials or listening history.

3. Data Sharing and Third Parties

CloudMix Pro does not sell, rent, trade, or share your Google user data with any third parties, advertisers, or analytics providers. Your data is used solely to provide DJ library and playback functionality on your own machine.

4. Google API Services User Data Policy

CloudMix Pro's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

5. Revoking Access & Deleting Data

You can sign out at any time in CloudMix Pro Settings (which deletes local OAuth tokens from ytm-auth.json), or revoke access directly from your Google Account Permissions page.
